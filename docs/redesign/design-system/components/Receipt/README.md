The printed receipt: an 80 mm thermal slip (72 mm printable) that belongs to the pharmacy and the customer, in solid black only.

**The consumer provides** everything from Settings and the sale — nothing on the slip is Mehvar's own except one optional line.

- **Header — the pharmacy's own,** as set in Settings: name (18px bold), address, phone, drug sale licence, then date and time. No logo of any kind: no Mehvar mark, and no images (thermal dithering).
- **Body:** QTY | ITEM | AMT columns, one unit per line ("2 strip"); Subtotal, item and Bill Discount rows, **Amount Due** in bold; then Paid, or the Cash and Udhaar split. Udhaar sales and settlements add the mini-statement: previous balance, this transaction, new balance.
- **Footer:** "Receipt 0412 · Served by Ali", the pharmacy's thank-you line, and last, one small line **"Powered by Mehvar"** (10px, black). It is on by default and the owner can switch it off in Settings → Receipt.
- Black on white only: no greys, tints or brand colour reach paper; rules are dashed black borders.
- Staff notices (a shortfall, an expired batch) never print; they belong on the counter screen and the Day exceptions.
